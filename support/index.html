MsaadaSupport

Maswali ya mara kwa maraFrequently asked questions

Programu inahitaji intaneti?Does the app need internet?

Hapana. Mada zote ziko ndani ya programu na haina ruhusa ya intaneti.

No. All topics are inside the app and it has no internet permission.

Taarifa zangu zinahifadhiwa wapi?Where is my data stored?

Kwenye simu yako tu. Angalia sera ya faragha.

On your phone only. See the privacy policy.

Ninawezaje kufuta taarifa zangu?How do I delete my data?

Fungua Msaada → Faragha yako → Futa taarifa zangu zote, au soma ukurasa wa kufuta taarifa.

Open Help → Your privacy → Delete all my data, or read the data deletion page.

Je, hii ni ushauri wa kisheria?Is this legal advice?

Hapana. Ni taarifa za jumla. Kwa kesi yako wasiliana na wakili.

No. It is general information. For your own case, contact a lawyer.

Nimepata kosa kwenye maudhui.I found a mistake in the content.

Tafadhali tuandikie ukitaja mada na kosa ulilooona ili tulirekebishe.

Please write to us with the topic and the mistake so we can correct it.

Unahitaji msaada wa kisheria wa haraka?Need urgent legal help?

Programu hii haitoi ushauri wa kisheria. Wasiliana na wakili au ofisi ya msaada wa kisheria iliyo karibu nawe, au kituo cha polisi ikiwa uko hatarini.
This app does not give legal advice. Contact a lawyer or a nearby legal aid office, or the police if you are in danger.

Mwongozo Wako ni mradi huru, hauhusiani na Serikali ya Jamhuri ya Muungano wa Tanzania. Inatoa taarifa za jumla za kisheria, si ushauri wa kisheria.Mwongozo Wako is an independent project, not affiliated with the Government of the United Republic of Tanzania. It provides general legal information, not legal advice.
