Kufuta taarifa zakoDeleting your data

Programu ya Mwongozo Wako haina akaunti wala seva, kwa hiyo hatuna taarifa zako upande wetu. Kila kitu kimehifadhiwa kwenye simu yako tu (mada ulizohifadhi, lugha, na historia ya utafutaji ukiiwasha).

Mwongozo Wako has no accounts and no servers, so we hold none of your data. Everything is stored on your phone only (saved topics, language, and search history if you turned it on).

Njia ya kufutaHow to delete

  1. Fungua programu.
  2. Nenda Msaada → Faragha yako.
  3. Gusa Futa taarifa zangu zote.

Vinginevyo, ondoa programu kwenye simu yako; taarifa zote zitafutwa.

  1. Open the app.
  2. Go to Help → Your privacy.
  3. Tap Delete all my data.

Alternatively, uninstall the app from your phone; all data is removed.
