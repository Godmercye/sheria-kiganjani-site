Familia na ndoaFamily and marriage

Ndoa na talakaMarriage and divorce

Talaka hutolewa na mahakama baada ya ndoa kuvunjika kabisa. Mgawanyo wa mali ya ndoa huzingatia mchango wa kila mwenzi.

Divorce is granted by a court once a marriage has broken down irreparably. Division of matrimonial property considers each spouse's contribution.

WatotoChildren

Mtoto ana haki ya kupata malezi, elimu na ulinzi dhidi ya ukatili. Maslahi ya mtoto huwa ya kwanza katika maamuzi ya malezi.

Every child has a right to care, education and protection from abuse. The child's best interests come first in custody decisions.

MirathiInheritance

Mirathi hufuata wosia ikiwa upo, au sheria ya mirathi inayotumika (ya kimila, ya dini au ya serikali) ikiwa haupo.

Estates follow a will if there is one; otherwise the applicable inheritance law (customary, religious or statutory) applies.

Sheria husika: Sheria ya Ndoa na Sheria ya Mtoto.Relevant laws: the Law of Marriage Act and the Law of the Child Act.
