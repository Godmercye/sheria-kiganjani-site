Ajira na kaziEmployment and work

Mkataba wa ajiraEmployment contract

Mwajiri anatakiwa kukupa maelezo ya maandishi ya masharti ya ajira yako, ikiwemo mshahara, saa za kazi na majukumu.

Your employer must give you written particulars of your job, including pay, working hours and duties.

LikizoLeave

Mfanyakazi ana haki ya likizo ya mwaka yenye malipo, likizo ya ugonjwa, na likizo ya uzazi kwa mujibu wa sheria.

Employees are entitled to paid annual leave, sick leave and maternity leave as provided by law.

Kuachishwa kaziTermination

Kuachishwa kazi lazima iwe kwa sababu halali na kwa utaratibu wa haki. Ukiona umeonewa, tafuta msaada wa Tume ya Usuluhishi na Uamuzi (CMA).

A dismissal must be for a valid reason and follow a fair procedure. If you think you were treated unfairly, seek help from the Commission for Mediation and Arbitration (CMA).

Sheria kuu: Sheria ya Ajira na Mahusiano Kazini.Main law: the Employment and Labour Relations Act.
