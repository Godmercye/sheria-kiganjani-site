Haki za mtumiajiConsumer rights

Bidhaa mbovuFaulty goods

Una haki ya bidhaa zenye ubora unaostahili. Hifadhi risiti na uwasiliane na muuzaji mapema.

You have the right to goods of acceptable quality. Keep your receipt and contact the seller promptly.

MalalamikoComplaints

Ukishindwa kupata suluhu kwa muuzaji, peleka malalamiko kwa Baraza la Ushindani wa Haki (FCC) au mamlaka ya sekta husika kama TCRA kwa huduma za simu.

If the seller does not resolve it, complain to the Fair Competition Commission (FCC) or the sector regulator, such as TCRA for telecom services.

Sheria husika: Sheria ya Ushindani wa Haki.Relevant law: the Fair Competition Act.
