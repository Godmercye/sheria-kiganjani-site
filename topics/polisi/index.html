Polisi na haki za mtuhumiwaPolice and rights of the accused

UkikamatwaIf arrested

Una haki ya kujulishwa sababu ya kukamatwa, kukaa kimya, na kuwasiliana na ndugu au wakili.

You have the right to be told why you are arrested, to remain silent, and to contact a relative or lawyer.

Muda wa kushikiliwaTime in custody

Kwa kawaida mtuhumiwa anatakiwa kufikishwa mahakamani ndani ya saa 24. Dhamana ni haki kwa makosa mengi.

A suspect should generally be brought before a court within 24 hours. Bail is available for most offences.

Msaada wa kisheriaLegal aid

Ikiwa huna uwezo wa kumlipa wakili, tafuta huduma za msaada wa kisheria zilizosajiliwa.

If you cannot afford a lawyer, look for registered legal aid providers.

Sheria husika: Katiba ya Jamhuri ya Muungano na Sheria ya Mwenendo wa Makosa ya Jinai.Relevant laws: the Constitution and the Criminal Procedure Act.

Hii ni taarifa ya jumla, si ushauri wa kisheria. Kwa kesi yako, wasiliana na wakili au ofisi ya msaada wa kisheria.This is general information, not legal advice. For your own case, contact a lawyer or legal aid office.
