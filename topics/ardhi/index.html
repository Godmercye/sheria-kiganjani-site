Ardhi na makaziLand and housing

UmilikiOwnership

Ardhi yote Tanzania ni mali ya umma, na watu hupewa haki za kumiliki. Hati au leseni ya makazi ni ushahidi muhimu wa haki yako.

All land in Tanzania is public land; individuals hold rights of occupancy. A title deed or residential licence is key evidence of your right.

UpangajiRenting

Weka mkataba wa upangaji wa maandishi na uhifadhi risiti za kodi. Mwenye nyumba hawezi kukutoa bila utaratibu wa kisheria.

Put your tenancy in writing and keep rent receipts. A landlord cannot evict you without following the legal process.

MigogoroDisputes

Migogoro mingi ya ardhi huanzia Baraza la Ardhi la Kata, kisha Baraza la Ardhi na Nyumba la Wilaya.

Most land disputes start at the Ward Tribunal, then the District Land and Housing Tribunal.

Sheria kuu: Sheria ya Ardhi na Sheria ya Ardhi ya Vijiji.Main laws: the Land Act and the Village Land Act.
