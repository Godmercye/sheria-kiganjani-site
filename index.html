Kiswahili

Kwa ufupi: Sheria Kiganjani haikusanyi, haitumi wala haiuzi taarifa zako binafsi. Sera hii imeandaliwa kwa mujibu wa Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi ya Tanzania ya mwaka 2022.

Tunachohifadhi

Mada ulizohifadhi, lugha uliyochagua, na historia ya utafutaji ukiiwasha tu (imezimwa mwanzoni). Taarifa hizi zinahifadhiwa kwenye simu yako tu.

Tusichofanya

Programu haina ruhusa ya intaneti, kwa hiyo haiwezi kutuma taarifa zako popote. Hakuna akaunti, matangazo, vifuatiliaji wala takwimu za matumizi. Hatuombi jina, namba ya simu, NIDA, mahali ulipo, picha wala anwani zako. Taarifa haziwekwi kwenye nakala ya mtandaoni (cloud backup).

Widget ya skrini ya nyumbani

Inasoma mada kutoka ndani ya programu tu na haitumi chochote.

Haki zako

Una haki ya kujua taarifa zinazohifadhiwa, kuzirekebisha na kuzifuta. Tumia "Msaada → Faragha yako → Futa taarifa zangu zote", au ondoa programu. Una haki pia ya kuwasilisha malalamiko kwa Tume ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi (PDPC).

Watoto

Programu hii imekusudiwa watu wazima na haikusanyi taarifa za mtu yeyote, wakiwemo watoto.

Mabadiliko

Tukiongeza huduma yoyote itakayokusanya taarifa, tutaomba ridhaa yako kwanza na kusasisha ukurasa huu.

Mawasiliano

English

In short: Sheria Kiganjani does not collect, send or sell your personal information. This policy is written under Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

What we store

Topics you save, your chosen language, and search history only if you turn it on (off by default). This is stored on your phone only.

What we don't do

The app has no internet permission, so it can't send your data anywhere. There are no accounts, ads, trackers or analytics. We never ask for your name, phone number, NIDA number, location, photos or contacts. Your data is excluded from cloud backup.

Home-screen widget

It only reads topics bundled inside the app and sends nothing.

Your rights

You have the right to know what is stored, to correct it and to delete it. Use "Help → Your privacy → Delete all my data", or uninstall the app. You also have the right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).

Children

The app is intended for adults and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we ever add a feature that collects data, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
