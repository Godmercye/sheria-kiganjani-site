Mwongozo Wako

Sheria za Tanzania, kiganjani mwako.Tanzanian law, in the palm of your hand.

Programu ya Android ya kuelewa haki zako kwa Kiswahili rahisi na Kiingereza. Haihitaji akaunti wala intaneti, na haikusanyi taarifa zako.An Android app to understand your rights in plain Swahili and English. No account, no internet permission, and it collects none of your data.
